## Out of scope

- Using the local driver on any Vercel environment or other shared deployment, or serving published gifts from it.
- Trigger.dev tasks (deployed or `pnpm jobs:dev`) against local storage.
- Range requests, `HEAD` downloads, caching and audio delivery through the local routes.
- Automatic pruning of `.tmp/object-storage/` beyond the existing cleanup and delete flows.
- Rendering template images from local storage in an E2E (`add-gift-preview`), and the full Gate M2 journey (`add-funnel-analytics`).
